How to Create Your Social Sharing Image

This preview shows a professional social sharing card design. To create the actual image file:

  1. Right-click on the card above
  2. Select "Save image as..." or "Copy image"
  3. Save it as social-share-card.jpg in your public/images/ folder
  4. Or use a screenshot tool to capture just the card area

Tip: This design follows Amy Porterfield's style with professional gradients, clear typography, and a strong call-to-action.
